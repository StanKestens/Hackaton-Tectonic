# Hackaton-Tectonic

## Problem Statement

Banks sit on a massive wealth of transactional data, but their approach to customer guidance remains stubbornly reactive. Traditional rule-based systems wait for customers to navigate the maze of banking, insurance, and investments on their own, or worse, bombard them with generic product banners that feel like spam. They don't detect the life events hidden in daily spending, they don't anticipate the next logical step, and they treat financial products as isolated silos. Customers don't need another standard app feature — they need an intelligent financial ecosystem that connects the dots of their life, anticipates what they need before they have to ask, and seamlessly guides them at the exact right moment.

Who benefits: The 2.3 million KBC customers who want proactive, context-aware support tailored to their specific life events rather than generic sales pitches — and the bank itself, which needs a scalable way to transform fragmented transactional data into meaningful, highly individualized relationships.
